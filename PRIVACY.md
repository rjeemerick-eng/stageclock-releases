# StageClock Viewer — Privacy Policy

**Effective: September 24, 2026**

StageClock Viewer is a display client for the StageClock production clock
system. It is built to show clocks — not to collect information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** StageClock Viewer collects no personal information, requires no
account, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

## What the app does on your network

The app communicates only with a StageClock operator computer that you choose,
on your own local network, to receive clock and rundown information. The
display name you enter exists so your operator's console can label this
screen; it is stored on your device and sent only to your operator.

No data leaves your local network. Nothing is transmitted to the developer or
to any third party.

## Data stored on the device

The app remembers your display name, your preferred view, and the address of
the operator you last connected to, so it can reconnect automatically. This
information stays on the device and can be cleared by deleting the app.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
